WhatsApp conversation pricing

Your 11za plan and WhatsApp charges are separate

Your subscription gives you access to the 11za platform. WhatsApp/Meta conversation charges may apply separately based on the applicable conversation category and rules.

01
Customer initiated

When a customer starts a conversation and your business responds within the applicable service window.

02
Business initiated

Business-initiated communication can create a charge depending on the applicable WhatsApp pricing rules.

03
Conversation category

Conversation charges depend on the applicable category and WhatsApp's current pricing model.
