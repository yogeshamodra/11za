Who we are

Making Business Communication Simple

11za is a technology platform built to help businesses manage and grow customer conversations on WhatsApp.

We started with a simple observation: as businesses grow, customer conversations grow with them. Managing those conversations across personal phones, spreadsheets and disconnected tools can quickly become difficult.

“

11za brings everything together in one place.

From customer enquiries and sales conversations to support, notifications, campaigns and follow-ups, businesses can use 11za to organize their communication, empower their teams and automate repetitive tasks.

Our focus is not just on technology. It's on solving real business problems with tools that are simple to use, easy to scale and built around the way businesses communicate today.

Whether you're a growing local business, an MSME or an established organization, 11za helps you turn everyday WhatsApp conversations into meaningful customer relationships.
